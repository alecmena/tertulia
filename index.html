TERTULIA

◆

ter·tu·lia, n. — a regular gathering of friends and strangers to talk, unhurried, of the arts, of ideas, and of the affairs of the day.

Tertulia is a monthly salon in Washington. Each evening draws a small company around a single question, chosen beforehand and sent ahead. We meet to think out loud, to disagree well, and to leave having listened as much as spoken. Conversation is the whole of the entertainment.
